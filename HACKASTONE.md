# hackAstone 2026: Synapse by Team EduNova

## Result

**Grand Final, 29 October 2026, Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, at the Conference on AI For Education The Netherlands 2026:**

- **5th place overall**
- **Best/favourite presentation award**

This repository is the closing record of the entry. It holds the build presented at the final, Synapse v4, with the event material in [`hackathon/`](hackathon/). It is frozen: development continues elsewhere.

## The entry

**Theme:** Agentic AI for Education.

**Team EduNova, Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS:** Ahmad Alif Fadhil (lead), Muhammad Khaidhir, Nur Farah, Por Jie Hao, Azrain Shawn, Fakhrul Azim, Adam Iman, Muhammad Danish Iskandar.

**Advisors:** Assoc. Prof. Ts. Dr. Ahmad Sobri bin Hashim, and Assoc. Prof. Ir. Ts. Dr. M Naufal M Saad as UTP's Steering Committee representative.

| Round | Format | Build |
|---|---|---|
| Preliminary | 8 minute video and written description | Synapse v1, kept in [synapse-v1](https://github.com/Azimlearning/synapse-v1) (private). Its commit is also in this repository's history |
| Grand Final | 10 minute presentation, 5 minute Q&A, staffed booth demo | Synapse v4, this repository |

Between the rounds the product changed from a tutor that picks pre-built components to one that writes a lab for the question and refuses to show it unless it passes checks in a real browser. See [`README.md`](README.md).
